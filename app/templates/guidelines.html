Community Standards & Public Figure Doctrine

VEKHU is an editorial index designed to measure, structure, and understand collective public sentiment around cultural, technological, and media figures. These policies define how we protect both honest critique and personal safety.

1. The Public Figure Boundary

PULSE indexes individuals who actively participate in public discourse, run public-facing institutions, create public media, or shape cultural and civic ideas.

  • Eligible Subjects: Technology founders, recognized creators/streamers, published authors, journalists, public executives, and cultural critics.
  • Strictly Prohibited Subjects: Private citizens, minors under 18, non-public coworkers, personal acquaintances, and local retail employees without a public platform.

Any profile created for a private individual is immediately expunged, and the submitting IP is permanently barred from creating profiles.

2. Honest Critique vs. Harassment & Defamation

We staunchly protect robust, critical, and challenging public opinion. However, we draw a hard line against malicious abuse:

✓ Permitted Opinions Critiques of leadership style, product decisions, artistic output, consistency, intellectual honesty, public statements, and business ethics.
✗ Strictly Prohibited Doxxing (private phone numbers, home addresses), violent threats, hate speech, allegations of criminal conduct without judicial record, and targeted harassment.

3. Anonymity & Anti-Brigading Mechanics

PULSE supports zero-friction anonymous submissions so contributors can speak truth to power without fear of professional or social retaliation.

To prevent coordinated review-bombing and bot astroturfing:

  • Each submission is linked to a one-way hashed IP address and unique browser session token.
  • Duplicate submissions from the same actor on the same figure are automatically rejected.
  • Spike-detection algorithms flag sudden anomalous surges in 1-star or 5-star votes for moderator inspection.

4. Profile Claims, Corrections & Right of Reply

Public figures or their verified representatives may claim their profile badge by contacting claims@pulse.local.

Verified figures receive an official badge, the ability to pin a single verified response at the top of their dossier, and direct access to perception analytics over time.

⚖ Non-Negotiable Standard: Claimed profiles CANNOT delete legitimate criticism or alter their 5-star distribution curve. Score manipulation cannot be purchased.
