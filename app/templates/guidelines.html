Trust, Safety & Honesty

Community Guidelines & Rules

VEKHU is built to provide honest, transparent ratings and reviews for public figures and creators. These rules protect genuine community critique while keeping everyone safe.

1. Only Public Figures Allowed

VEKHU is strictly for individuals who have an active public audience, create public content, run public companies, or participate in public culture:

  • Allowed: Tech founders, YouTube/streamer creators, authors, journalists, public executives, and artists.
  • Strictly Forbidden: Private individuals, minors under 18, private coworkers, personal friends, or family.

Any profile created for a private individual is immediately deleted, and the submitting user is banned.

2. Honest Critique vs. Harassment

We encourage honest, critical, and direct reviews. However, we do not tolerate abuse or dangerous behavior:

✓ Allowed Critiques of leadership, product quality, video content, public statements, work ethics, and business decisions.
✗ Strictly Forbidden Doxxing (sharing private addresses, phone numbers), violent threats, hate speech, slurs, and false criminal allegations.

3. Anonymous & Free from Retaliation

VEKHU allows free, anonymous reviews so anyone can share their real thoughts without fear of social or professional backlash.

To prevent bots and review bombing:

  • Each review is checked with secure spam protection to prevent duplicate votes.
  • Bot networks and coordinated attacks are automatically detected and filtered out.

4. Right of Reply & Profile Claims

If you are a public figure and want to claim your official profile badge or submit an official reply, contact our team at contact@vekhu.com.

⚖ Important: Public figures CANNOT pay to delete negative reviews or change their community star ratings. VEKHU ratings are 100% independent.
